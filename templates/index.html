Frequently asked questions

How do I add the chatbot to my website? +

You paste one small script tag into your website. If you use WordPress, Shopify or Wix, we can do it for you.

What if the bot doesn't know the answer? +

It won't make things up. It asks for the visitor's contact details and alerts your team so a real person can follow up.

How long does setup take? +

Most businesses are live within a week, once we have your services and common questions.

What happens to my customers' data? +

Conversations are stored so you can review them, and visitors are told that the chat is saved. Read our Privacy Policy or contact us for details.
